## 1. Brand Strategic Evaluation: "SurePrice" vs. "Qarty"

### Why the Shift to "Qarty" Works (Marketing & Psychology Audit)

| Dimension | "SurePrice" (Old Name) | "Qarty" (New Name) | Verdict & Advantage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Category Perception** | Sounds like a discount comparison tool or bargain site (e.g. PriceCheck, PricePally). | Sounds like a modern retail-tech platform (like Shopify, Klarna, Chowdeck, Bumpa). | **Qarty wins.** Removes the cheap/discount stigma; positions you as modern in-store infrastructure. |
| **Subconscious Association** | "Sure" (Certainty) + "Price" (Cost/Expense). | **Q** (QR / Quick / Quality) + **Cart** (Commerce / Shopping) + **Party** (Fun / Social / Lively). | **Qarty wins.** High cognitive fluency; feels light, friendly, and consumer-inviting. |
| **Future Product Scope** | Traps you strictly into price display. Cannot expand to menus, ordering, or loyalty without friction. | Broad enough to cover price tags, restaurant digital menus, table ordering, scan analytics, and retail discovery. | **Qarty wins.** Unlocks horizontal expansion across hospitality, fashion, groceries, and events. |
| **Spoken Clarity & Radio Test** | Highly descriptive, easy to spell on first hearing. | Phonetically sounds like "Carty". Risk: People might search with a "C" if heard in conversation. | **Tie.** Mitigation: Always spell it out: *"Qarty with a Q — Q-A-R-T-Y"*, and secure search/redirects. |
| **Visual Footprint on Acrylic Tags** | Long text string (`SurePrice`). Takes up shelf tag margin. | Short, 5-letter brand (`Qarty`). Leaves ample whitespace for high-contrast QR code and price display. | **Qarty wins.** Much better ergonomics on tiny $70 \times 40\text{mm}$ shelf rails. |

> [!TIP]
> **The Golden Rule for "Qarty": Never run the logo naked.**  
> Because "Qarty" is an invented brand name, pair it with a 3-word functional descriptor on every physical banner, table standee, and handout:  
> **QARTY** — *1-Tap In-Store QR Tags & Menus*
