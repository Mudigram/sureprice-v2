# SurePrice Promo — First Launch Video

**Duration:** ~20 seconds  
**Format:** 1080×1920 (9:16 vertical, mobile-first)  
**Audience:** Shoppers and merchant pilot partners in Nigeria  
**Tagline:** Scan it. Know it.

## Creative direction

Electric marketplace aesthetic: midnight slate canvas (`#020617`) with neon lime (`#13ec5b`) reserved for CTAs and verified price signals. Crisp Inter typography, polished anime.js-driven motion, no slide-deck fades. Text punctuates; visuals carry the story.

## Verified product features (from landing + app copy)

1. **Zero-friction scan** — shoppers point any smartphone camera at a shelf QR tag; no app download required
2. **Verified Naira (₦) pricing** — instant price display in Nigerian Naira
3. **Sub-second load** — webview resolves in ~0.4s average
4. **1-tap cloud price sync** — merchants update prices across locations instantly
5. **Durable waterproof QR hardware** — acrylic shelf tags, restaurant tent cards, festival stall passes
6. **Venues** — supermarkets, dining cafés, pop-up vendors across Nigeria
